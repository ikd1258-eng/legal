## Common questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial ends, and you won't be charged.

**How do I restore a purchase?**
Tap "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen. Purchases made with the same Apple Account come back.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**How do I stop sharing analytics?**
Turn off Share Analytics on the You page in the App at any time.

**What happens to my garden on a new iPhone?**
If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud backup, your garden, Rest Day Passes and settings come with it. Your check-in answers and the values read from Apple Health stay on the old device.
