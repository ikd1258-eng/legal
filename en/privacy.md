# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Takeru Ikeda ("we") handles information in Soyoro, our iPhone app (the App).

## 1. Our approach

The App keeps your sitting records and body data on your device only. It doesn't send them to our servers, and we never see them. The App contains no advertising. Only if you turn on Share Analytics does the App send counts of how it's used to an analytics service (see 3-3).

## 2. What the App uses and why

| Information | What it's used for | Where it's kept |
|---|---|---|
| Days you sat, how long, the menu you chose, your goal in days, how many Rest Day Passes you have | To show your garden, streak, calendar, and suggestions | On your device |
| Your device's movement while you sit (motion sensors) | To draw the patterns in the sand | On your device. The motion values are saved with your sitting record so the lines in the sand can be redrawn. They're never sent off your device |
| Your answers to the daily check-in | To show the graphs in your garden and today's suggestion | On your device |
| Heart rate from Health (read only if you allow it) | To show your heart rate before and after sitting in that day's record | In Health and on your device. Never sent off your device |
| Sleep from Health (read only if you allow it) | To show your nightly sleep beside your garden and that day's record, and to set how much moss grows on a night's stone | In Health and on your device. Never sent off your device |
| Mindful Minutes in Health (written only if you allow it) | To record your sitting time | In Health |
| Notification permission | To show notices, such as when your free trial is ending, sent from your device itself | On your device |
| Saving to Photos (only if you allow it) | To save the garden images or videos you choose | In your photo library |

We never use information read from Health for advertising, for sale, or for any purpose not listed in this table.

## 3. Purchase information

Purchases, billing, cancellation, and refunds for the subscription and Rest Day Passes are handled through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details (such as card numbers). The App checks on your device whether a purchase is active, using Apple's StoreKit. For how Apple handles your information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 3-2. Information we receive

The only information we receive is email you send us (your name, email address, and what you write). Apple may also send us aggregated sales figures that don't identify you, and crash or problem reports that you choose to share.

| Information | Purpose and legal basis | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Emails you send us | To answer your questions (performance of a contract for questions about purchases or contracts; our legitimate interests for all other questions) | 1 year after your question is resolved. If an email mentions your health or similar, it isn't stored separately |

We handle your emails in Japan. The European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision for Japan, recognizing that it provides an adequate level of data protection.

Information on your device (sitting records, motion, heart rate, and so on) never reaches us, so we can't see it or delete it. To delete it, delete the App, or delete it in the Health app.

The App doesn't make automated decisions about you or profile you.

## 3-3. Share Analytics (only if you turn it on)

Only if you turn on Share Analytics, the App sends counts of how it's used to an analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH (Augsburg, Germany), so we can improve the App.

- The App sends one report the first time you open it each day, and a few when you view the purchase screen. Restoring purchases, buying Rest Day Passes, and changes to your subscription status are sent together in the next day's single report. A report contains: how many days since you started and the week you started; whether you have the subscription; whether notifications are allowed; the App and iOS versions; your country or region; how many times you sat the day before and how long, in ranges; how many of the last 7 days you sat; your streak length, in ranges; whether, the day before, your streak ended, a Rest Day Pass was used, a streak was brought back, or a milestone was reached; whether you answered today's check-in; where the purchase screen was shown and what happened there; whether you tapped Restore Purchases; whether you bought a Rest Day Pass; and changes to your subscription status.
- The identifier sent with each report is a random number this device makes anew every day. It isn't linked to the previous day's number, or to your name, email address, or advertising identifier. We don't follow the same person from one day to the next.
- The App doesn't send your local time, the day of the week, or the times you sat. The service does record when it received each report (the time you first opened the App that day, and the times you opened the purchase screen).
- The App never sends your mood or check-in answers, the purposes you choose, the menus you choose, motion, heart rate, or values from Health.
- When data is sent, your device's IP address reaches the service as part of how the internet works. For how the service handles it, see TelemetryDeck's privacy policy (https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).
- The data sent is kept under the service's own rules. Because it can't be told apart person by person, we can't delete one person's data once it has been sent.
- You can turn off Share Analytics at any time on the You page in the App. When you do, the App stops sending from that moment and deletes anything not yet sent.
- This feature isn't used in the App Store in the EU, the EEA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway), the UK, or Switzerland.

On your device, the App stores only what the features you use need and, if you turn on Share Analytics, the identifier and the counts not yet sent.

## 4. Sharing

Images and videos of your garden go only to the app or person you choose, and only when you choose to share them.

## 5. Disclosure to third parties

We don't provide your information to third parties, except as required by law.

## 6. Storage and deletion

Your information is stored on your device. So you can keep your garden when you change phones, your garden records (when you sat, for how long, which menu, and the lines in the sand), your Rest Day Passes, and your App settings are included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup). Backups are handled by Apple and never reach us. Your mood and check-in answers, your answers to the first questions, and values read from Health are not included in backups. They stay on this device only.

## 7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

## 8. Your rights

For the information we hold about you (emails you send us), you can ask us to give you access to it, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to its processing, or give you a copy to take elsewhere (data portability). Contact us at the email address below.

If you live in the EU, you can also lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country (list: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en ).

## 9. Changes

When we change this policy, we'll let you know in the App or on this page.
